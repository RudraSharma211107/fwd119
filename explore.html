About Our Portal

The SAU Security Portal is a digital platform developed to simplify the management of security personnel and campus duty operations. It provides a unified system for managing staff records, monitoring attendance, organising duty assignments, maintaining schedules and sharing important notices. The portal aims to improve coordination, accessibility and efficiency in university security management.

Portal Features

The portal provides several features to manage different aspects of university security operations.

👥

Security Staff Management

Maintain records of security personnel, including their names, personnel IDs, contact details, designations and supervisors.

📋

Attendance Management

Record and monitor daily attendance of security personnel with statuses such as Present, Absent, Late and On Leave.

📍

Duty Assignments

Assign security personnel to specific campus buildings, floors and departments with designated shifts and timings.

📅

Schedule Management

Organise weekly and monthly duty schedules, shift timings and upcoming assignments for security personnel.

📝

Leave Management

Allow security personnel to submit leave applications and enable administrators to review, approve or reject requests.

📢

Notices and Announcements

Publish important instructions, duty schedule changes and general announcements for security personnel.

📊

Reports and Overview

Help administrators review attendance records, staff information and duty operations through organised reports.
